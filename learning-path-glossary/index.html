Learning path and glossary

A staged plan for becoming fluent in omics analysis, followed by the vocabulary you will meet in papers and pipeline documentation.

Stages

StageBiologyComputationStatisticsPractice project
1 · FoundationsChapters 1–4 of this guide; a molecular biology textbook (Alberts, Molecular Biology of the Cell)Linux shell, file formats, git; R basics (tidyverse) or Python (pandas)Distributions, hypothesis tests, regression, p-values vs effect sizesDownload a GEO count matrix, make PCA and a volcano plot
2 · One layer wellGene regulation; the biology of your systemRun nf-core/rnaseq; Bioconductor (SummarizedExperiment) or scverse (AnnData)GLMs, negative binomial, multiple testing, empirical BayesReanalyse a published RNA-seq study end to end and compare with the paper
3 · Single-cell and a second layerCell types, development, chromatinScanpy or Seurat; ATAC or proteomics pipelines; HPC job schedulersDimensionality reduction, clustering, mixed models, compositional dataIntegrate two scRNA-seq datasets and test pseudobulk DE
4 · Integration and geneticsPopulation genetics, QTLsPLINK, tensorQTL, MOFA+, mixOmicsLatent factor models, Bayesian inference, causal inference (MR, colocalisation)Colocalise a GWAS with GTEx eQTLs; run MOFA+ on TCGA multi-omics
5 · Methods developmentDeep knowledge of one systemPyTorch/JAX, package development, testing, benchmarking frameworksProbabilistic modelling, variational inference, ML evaluationBuild and benchmark a method against neutral baselines

Recommended resources

Books

  • Alberts et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell
  • Strachan & Read, Human Molecular Genetics
  • Holmes & Huber, Modern Statistics for Modern Biology (free online)
  • James et al., An Introduction to Statistical Learning (free online)
  • Durbin et al., Biological Sequence Analysis

Online and free

  • Single-cell Best Practices (sc-best-practices.org)
  • Orchestrating Single-Cell Analysis with Bioconductor (OSCA)
  • Bioconductor workflows and vignettes
  • Galaxy Training Network
  • nf-core documentation
  • GATK Best Practices documentation

Habits

  • Reanalyse published data before generating your own
  • Read the methods section of every tool you use
  • Keep a lab notebook for analyses (date, versions, decisions)
  • Follow benchmarks and preprints in your area (bioRxiv)
  • Join communities: Biostars, Bioconductor support, scverse forum
