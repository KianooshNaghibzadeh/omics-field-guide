<h2>About this guide</h2>
<p>The Omics Field Guide is a free, independent resource for students and researchers starting out in omics and bioinformatics. If you find an error or an outdated benchmark, please send an email with the chapter name and a reference, and it will be corrected in the next edition.</p>
<p>If you use this guide in teaching or a publication, please cite it as: <em>Naghibzadeh K. The Omics Field Guide. 2026. https://kianooshnaghibzadeh.github.io/omics-field-guide/</em></p>
